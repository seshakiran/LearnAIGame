# Draft TestFlight copy

## Beta description
Last Train is a fictional interactive subway thriller about judging AI-generated information. Work from transit command, inspect records, reconstruct events, and help a team distinguish evidence from confident assumptions. No prior AI knowledge is required.

This is an early mobile-first learning prototype. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NYPD or MTA, and is not emergency-response training.

## What to test
Start with Chapter 1. Choose a lead, assemble the timeline, and send your briefing. Tell us where text feels too small, where the story slows down, whether choices feel meaningful, and whether you want to continue. Please include your device model and chapter/scene title in feedback. Try closing and reopening the app halfway through the timeline puzzle.

## Beta review notes
The game requires no account or login. All incident records, messages and characters are authored fictional content. There is no live AI service in this build. Art, music and story content are bundled. Progress is stored locally. The story contains suspense and non-graphic references to coercion and crime. Final age-rating answers must be completed against the whole app.

Owner-supplied beta feedback email, contact information, privacy disclosures and final age rating remain pending.
