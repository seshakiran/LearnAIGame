## Generation prompt

Create a **15-second vertical 9:16 micro-explainer** for a polished mobile game about AI judgment. Use one calm, credible adult narrator or an abstract game-guide silhouette against a dark-indigo, minimal interface-inspired background. The narrator delivers the voiceover **exactly as supplied below**, at an easy, natural pace with short pauses. Use restrained editorial motion graphics: first show a simple answer card, then word-prediction animation, then a stylized two-option card with `1889` contrasted against `1887, based on a blueprint...`. Emphasize that extra detail can feel credible without being true. Use large, highly legible off-white text only: `NOT LOOKING UP FACTS`, `PREDICTING WHAT SOUNDS LIKELY`, `EXTRA DETAIL ≠ EXTRA TRUTH`, `NOT INTENTIONAL`, `SOUNDING RIGHT ISN'T BEING RIGHT`. The mood is dry, clear, and mildly wry — never hype-driven, classroom-like, or comedic. Do not show logos, real documents, fake citations, real people, or any additional factual claims. Leave visual breathing room for mobile safe areas. End on a clean dark background.
