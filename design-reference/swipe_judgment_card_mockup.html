Material recipe No new hues
Card face, back to front
01 — Rear stack
Scaled 97%, offset +20 pt. Signals a deck without distracting from the active call.
02 — Lift and shadow
Soft blue atmospheric lift, then 26 pt far shadow and 11 pt contact shadow in deepest navy.
03 — Contour and recess
A 1.5 pt warm-white contour sits over a 1.5 pt dark edge recess. This is the tactile edge.
04 — Gradient face
Quiet slate at top, stable `#16232F` reading plane, deepest navy behind decision chips.
05 — Raised choices
Chips repeat the contour/recess/face stack at a smaller radius, with directional hue only at the perimeter.
Fixed visual states
At rest Neutral
All layers present, no answer preferred. Accent contours stay at 64% and both stamps remain invisible.
Dragging toward A Direction only
A’s chip gains a local halo and 2.5% scale. The blue stamp appears at 96% opacity while the card tilts 3.2° left.
Dragging toward B Direction only
B’s response mirrors A exactly. Amber identifies the direction, not the answer’s quality, correctness, or safety.
Unity translation anchors

Build depth by nesting rounded images.

Root movementOne card parent

Apply drag translation, tilt, and dismiss motion at the card root so face, chips, and stamp travel as one object.

Stroke methodOuter image + inset

Use a 1.5–3 pt inset dark image over a colored outer image. It creates contours with the existing procedural rounded sprite.

Feedback mapping140 pt to full stamp

Use smoothstep on horizontal drag magnitude. Never reveal both stamps, and never bring in correctness colors during drag.

Interaction safetyDecorations ignore raycasts

Set every shadow, glow, contour, and halo layer to ignore raycasts; retain gesture handling only on the required card surface.
